Kosi Geet

Kosi Bharai is a thanksgiving ritual for a wish granted by Chhathi Maiya. Women gather around a canopy of sugarcane, light diyas, and sing Kosi geet together.

Sugarcane canopy5 or 7 stalks tied together overhead
